H/W devices:
============

  Valve electrical:
  =================
    CR01, 12V, DN20(3/4) Model: CWX-25S
    CR01 - не потребляет ток ни в открытом не в закрытом положении. Управляется 2мя проводами сменой полярности.
    Power supply: 12V
	Power: <2W
    Working current: <80mA
  
  Water Flow Sensor:
  ==================
    YF-B10 1" 2-50L DC3.5~24V 476 pulse/L
    Power supply: n/a
  
  Pressure Sensor electrical:
  ===========================
    Model: SWP-300  0-5V G1-2, 0-10bar
    Connection: 1/2" Father
    Power supply: 12V-36VDC(default 24VDC)
    Signal: 0-5V
  
  Pressure Sensor mechanical:
  ===========================
    Connection: 1/4" Father
  
Power Consumption:
==================
   8 mA   - DC-DC 12V->5V Idle mode (LED)
  70 mA   - MC, Controller electronics
  18 mA / 12V - Direction Relay, after DC-DC 12V->5V - 30mA/5V
  25 mA / 12V - Power Relay, after DC-DC 12V->5V - 50mA/5V
  80 mA   - Electrical Valve CR01
  50 mA   - Pressure Sensor electrical
          - Система полива, Netafim Valves

MC:
===
  ESP32-WROOM-32U with external antena
  Power: 5V (5V is used to reuse the same voltage as for Pressure Sensor)


  GPIO:
  =====
  GPIO 21+ (SDA)  - LogicLevel LV2 -> HV2 -> SDA ADS1115
  GPIO 22+ (SCL)  - LogicLevel LV1 -> HV1 -> SCL ADS1115
  GPIO 32+        - LED: LED1 WiFi/MQTT Status (Blue) (pull-down)
  GPIO 17+        - Water Flow sensor (interrupt attached)               <<- Should be used some pull-up/down resistor?
  GPIO ??         - AC Power detected (in case of UPS used) (pool-up)    <<- What is it??
  GPIO 19+        - Valve dirrection (pool-down)                         HIGH: Open      LOW: Close
  GPIO 18+        - Valve power on (pool-down)                           HIGH: Power-ON  LOW: Power-OFF
  GPIO ??         - Water Pipe temperature (DS1824)
  GPIO ??         - Ambient temperature (DS1824)
  GPIO ??         - Сенсор протечки
  GPIO ??         - Button to open valve                                 <<- Check of the pure INPUT GPIO can be used
  GPIO ??         - Button to close valve                                <<- Check of the pure INPUT GPIO can be used
  GPIO ??         - Spare KEY1 Button                                    <<- Check of the pure INPUT GPIO can be used
  GPIO 33+        - LED: LED2 Water Valve Status (Green) (pull-down)
  GPIO 25+        - LED: LED3 Water Flow Rate (Yellow) (pull-down)
  GPIO 26+        - LED: LED4 Error (Red)  (pull-down)

  
  
Requirements:
=============
  Valve:
  ======
    > Open/Close Electrical Water Valve MQTT               (2 GPIO OUTPUT)
	> Физическая кнопка открытия/закрытия крана
	> Физический переключатель блокирования открытия крана
    > Ability to open/close Electrical Water Valve without MC (phisical buttons)
  Water Flow Sensor:
  ==================
    > Read Water Flow Sensor periodic                        (1 GPIO INPUT)
  Water Presure Sensor:
  =====================
    > Read Water Presure Sensor periodic (1m/15 sec)
    > Realtime Water Presure Sensor read
  Temperatures:
  =============
    > Read Water Pipe temperature
    > Read ambient temperature
    > ESP chip internal Temperature
  Notifications:
  ==============
    - MQTT connected
	- Water Flow Rate
	- Water Presure Rate
	- Протечка
	- Temperature
  > Кнопка включения AP/конфигурации
  
  > LED: WiFi/MQTT Status   LED1 (Blue)    - WiFi/MQTT status OFF: WiFi is not connected, Blinking: WiFi connected but MQTT is not connected, ON: WiFi+MQTT connected 
  > LED: Water Valve Status LED2 (Green)   - ON:valve open, OFF: valve closed, Blonking: valve opening/closing
  > LED: Water Flow Rate    LED3 (Yellow)  - blinking according to flow rate
  > LED: Error              LED4 (Red)     - Error / Malfunction detection
  > LED: Power              LED5 (???)     - Power is ON. GPIO not used
 
  Extension:

  > Включение/выключение полива (2 крана)
  > Read уровень влажности
  > Read Mechanical Water Presure Sensor + temperature periodic (1m/15 sec)
  > Подключение второго ESP (1 Serial 2 2 GPIO ???)
